📷 Camera tuning tips
  • Sit 1–1.5 m from the camera, head and shoulders in view.
  • Plain, light wall behind you. Light on your face, not behind you.
  • A dark shirt makes the ECB outline much clearer.
  • If the ECB picture is too busy, lower Grey levels to 3. If you vanish into the wall, raise it to 5.
  • If edges flicker, raise the Flicker margin to 8–10.
  • Camera not working? Pick Synthetic scene — the demo works the same.
